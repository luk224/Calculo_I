Ejemplo 4.27 de U · $f(x,y)=\ln(x^2+e^y)$
  1. Primeras: $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}=\frac{2x}{x^2+e^y}$, $\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}=\frac{e^y}{x^2+e^y}$.
  2. Segundas: $\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x^2}=\frac{2e^y-2x^2}{(x^2+e^y)^2}$; $\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x\partial y}=\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial y\partial x}=\frac{-2xe^y}{(x^2+e^y)^2}$; $\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial y^2}=\frac{x^2e^y}{(x^2+e^y)^2}$.
  3. En $(1,0)$: $Hf(1,0)=\begin{pmatrix}0&-\tfrac12\\-\tfrac12&\tfrac14\end{pmatrix}$.
